UPSC Prelims Syllabus - History & Art & Culture
================================================

UPSC Prelims questions are framed directly from these exact government
publications, reports, and foundational textbooks.

NCERTs:
- Class 11: An Introduction to Indian Art (Part 1)
- Class 12: Themes in Indian History (Parts I, II, & III)
- Old NCERT Class 11: Ancient India (R.S. Sharma)
- Old NCERT Class 11: Medieval India (Satish Chandra)
- Old NCERT Class 12: Modern India (Bipan Chandra)

Public Domain / Government Sources:
- CCRT Website (ccrtindia.gov.in; Art & Culture material)
- Ministry of Culture Press Releases (World Heritage Sites, Archaeological
  Survey of India findings)
