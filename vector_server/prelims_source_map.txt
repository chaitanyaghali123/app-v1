UPSC Prelims Source Map
========================

Stick exclusively to NCERTs and official government public-domain sources.
UPSC Prelims questions are framed directly from these exact government
publications, reports, and foundational textbooks.

Exact breakdown of NCERTs and official public-domain sources for each of the
7 Prelims GS Paper I subjects:

1. Polity & Governance
   NCERTs:
   - Class 11: Indian Constitution at Work
   - Class 11: Political Theory
   Public Domain / Government Sources:
   - The Constitution of India (Bare Act) (indiacode.nic.in)
   - PRS Legislative Research (summaries of newly passed Acts & Cabinet Bills)

2. History & Art & Culture
   NCERTs:
   - Class 11: An Introduction to Indian Art (Part 1)
   - Class 12: Themes in Indian History (Parts I, II, & III)
   - Old NCERT Class 11: Ancient India (R.S. Sharma)
   - Old NCERT Class 11: Medieval India (Satish Chandra)
   - Old NCERT Class 12: Modern India (Bipan Chandra)
   Public Domain / Government Sources:
   - CCRT Website (ccrtindia.gov.in; Art & Culture material)
   - Ministry of Culture Press Releases (World Heritage Sites, Archaeological
     Survey of India findings)

3. Geography
   NCERTs:
   - Class 11: Fundamentals of Physical Geography
   - Class 11: India: Physical Environment
   - Class 12: Fundamentals of Human Geography
   - Class 12: India: People and Economy
   Public Domain / Government Sources:
   - Bhuvan (ISRO Geo-portal) / Survey of India publication data

4. Economy
   NCERTs:
   - Class 11: Indian Economic Development
   - Class 12: Introductory Macroeconomics
   Public Domain / Government Sources:
   - Economic Survey of India (Ministry of Finance)
   - Union Budget Document (Ministry of Finance)
   - NITI Aayog Reports & Policy Papers (niti.gov.in)
   - RBI Annual Report / RBI Bulletin (basic monetary & banking concepts)

5. Environment & Ecology
   NCERTs:
   - Class 12 Biology NCERT: Chapters 13, 14, 15, and 16 (Ecology Unit)
   - Class 11 Chemistry NCERT: Environmental Chemistry chapter
   Public Domain / Government Sources:
   - MoEFCC (Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change) updates
   - India State of Forest Report (ISFR)
   - ENVIS Network (Environmental Information System)

6. General Science & Technology
   NCERTs:
   - Class 6 to 10: Science NCERTs (all base concepts)
   - Class 11 & 12: Selected chapters on Biotechnology, Semiconductors, and
     Communications
   Public Domain / Government Sources:
   - ISRO Official Releases (Space tech developments)
   - DRDO Releases (Defense technology)
   - Department of Biotechnology (DBT) updates

7. Current Affairs
   Public Domain / Government Sources:
   - PIB (Press Information Bureau) (pib.gov.in)
   - Yojana & Kurukshetra Magazines (Publications Division, Ministry of I&B)
   - India Year Book (Publications Division, Government of India)

Benefits for the App
--------------------
- Zero Copyright Issues: everything is in the public domain or freely
  published by the Government of India / NCERT.
- True UPSC Source Accuracy: questions created from these raw texts will
  match the language, facts, and scope that UPSC setters actually pull from.
